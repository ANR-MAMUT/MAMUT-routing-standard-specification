\section{Purpose and scope}
A MAMUT-routing benchmark specifies the data and arithmetic needed to determine whether submitted routes can serve every customer and what objective value those routes attain. Static instances describe each directed arc by its cost. Time-Dependent (TD) instances describe it by an Arrival Time Function (ATF). In both cases the benchmark fixes the binary64 inputs, the checker evaluates the declared objective exactly, and the result carries a separate value for display. This specification covers single-depot routing with homogeneous vehicle capacity and mandatory visits, with or without service-start windows. This edition is under coauthor review.

The default profiles are \id{binary64-exact-v1} for static instances and \id{td-binary64-exact-v1} for TD instances. Other readings take the decimal text itself as exact or restrict the operands to an integer grid. Other evaluators keep the binary64 reading and round during the calculation. These choices affect different steps in the four contracts below. Appendix~\ref{app:alternatives} lists their identifiers for readers encountering them in a manifest or historical record.

\subsection{Binary64 values, arithmetic and JSON}
JSON represents a number as text \autocite{brayJSON2017}. For an arc weight, a knot coordinate, a time or a fixed vehicle charge, interpret the token at its exact decimal value and round once to the nearest IEEE~754 binary64 value, breaking midpoint ties toward the even significand. Require a finite result, preserve subnormal values and identify signed zero with zero. Indices and counts are structural integers. Customer demands and capacity are nonnegative integers exactly representable in binary64.

Every finite binary64 value has the form $m/2^k$ for integers $m$ and $k\geq 0$. For example, the JSON token \id{0.1} supplies the exact operand $3602879701896397/36028797018963968$. In Python, \id{Fraction.from_float(0.1)} gives that fraction, while \id{Fraction("0.1")} gives $1/10$ \autocite{pythonFractions2026}. The distinction affects both a static arc cost and a TD knot. After loading, the checker computes in rational arithmetic. For example, a static sum of such operands is still dyadic, whereas interpolating between integer TD knots $(0,2)$ and $(3,4)$ at departure 1 gives the exact arrival $8/3$.
