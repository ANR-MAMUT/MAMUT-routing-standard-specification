\section{Other readings and evaluation modes}\label{app:alternatives}
A benchmark revision fixes its own profile. Comparing or replaying a result requires that identifier. The following names distinguish alternatives encountered in the static companion and historical TD records.
\begin{description}
\item[Integral binary64.] \id{integer-exact-v1} restricts static arc and temporal operands to integers. On eligible instances, including the integral DIMACS and ORTEC families, it produces the same values as \id{binary64-exact-v1}.
\item[Decimal tokens.] \id{decimal-exact-v1} takes the exact decimal value. The grid variant, \id{decimal-grid-3-v1}, requires multiples of $10^{-3}$.
\item[Arithmetic rounded to binary64.] \id{canonical-fp64-flat-v1} accumulates arcs in canonical route order. \id{canonical-fp64-grouped-v1} rounds route subtotals before summing them. \id{submitted-fp64-flat-v1} accumulates in submitted route order.
\item[Released TD checkers.] \id{td-fold/1} and \id{td-fold/2} identify historical TD evaluation contracts. Use the matching release and inputs to replay their results.
\end{description}
